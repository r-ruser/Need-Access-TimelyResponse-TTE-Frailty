# Figure contracts

All figures are descriptive presentations of prespecified observational analyses. They do not represent results from a randomized policy implementation trial. HRS and CLHLS are never contrasted as causal health-system effects.

## Figure_construct_decomposition

- Claim: the HRS Early-versus-Persistent estimate is more sensitive to the frailty outcome construct than to the included Need domains.
- Data: HRS 2 x 2 construct decomposition and person-bootstrap estimate shifts.
- Encodings: points and 95% confidence intervals for risk differences; percentage-point scale; zero reference line.
- Comparisons: A-D construct estimates and the four prespecified shift contrasts.
- Uncertainty: clustered robust intervals for construct estimates; person-bootstrap intervals for shifts.
- Limitation: shifts are sensitivity contrasts, not causal interactions.

## Figure_need_components

- Claim: the estimated policy effect varies across actionable-Need specifications, with weak support for some single-domain definitions.
- Data: seven HRS Need definitions in the primary population.
- Encodings: Early-versus-Persistent risk differences and 95% confidence intervals; filled points pass all gates, open points fail one or more gates.
- Limitation: this is construct sensitivity, not a ranking of behaviours.

## Figure_need_access_heatmap

- Claim: timely-response coverage and subsequent risk can be localized across Need burden and observable Access contexts.
- Data: HRS weighted Need x Access cells.
- Encodings: colour is Early-response percentage; labels give number of people, Early-response percentage and frailty-or-death percentage.
- Limitation: sparse or structurally absent cells are left blank and should not be interpreted.

## Figure_access_response

- Claim: among older adults standardized to comparable Need and covariates, Early-response probabilities differ modestly across measured Access contexts.
- Data: HRS and CLHLS marginal standardization models.
- Encodings: probability difference (advantaged/level 1 minus reference/level 0), 95% person-bootstrap interval.
- Limitation: unsupported indicators are labelled not estimable; cohort differences are descriptive.

## Figure_policy_coverage

- Claim: modeled increases in Early-response coverage yield small, uncertain changes in frailty-or-death and hospitalization under the observed outcome-risk pattern.
- Data: HRS observational policy-impact scenarios with person bootstrap.
- Encodings: expected risk and absolute event difference per 1,000 Need-eligible older adults.
- Limitation: scenario analysis is not a policy implementation trial.

## Figure_cross_cohort_transportability

- Claim: harmonizing the outcome construct moves the HRS estimate toward the common-construct direction; HRS and CLHLS common analyses point in a similar direction with different precision.
- Data: HRS A, B and D construct estimates plus CLHLS common-construct external replication.
- Encodings: staged construct pathway and common-construct forest plot, both on risk-difference percentage-point scale.
- Limitation: the cohort contrast is descriptive heterogeneity, not a causal health-system comparison.
